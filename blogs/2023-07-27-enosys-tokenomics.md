Introduction The design of a healthy cryptocurrency ecosystem consists of multiple vectors, one of which is the macroeconomic design of its native tokens, aka the tokenomics. As the traditional theory of economics and fiat currency regulations do not apply to cryptocurrencies, the dynamics of these systems are typically designed in an ad-hoc fashion, potentially leading to unstable situations and damaging the system as a whole. Ēnosys’ overarching goal is to mitigate potential risks stemming from this arbitrary design of tokenomics.
