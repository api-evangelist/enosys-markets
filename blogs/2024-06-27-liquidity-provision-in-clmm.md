Providing liquidity in Enosys DEX V3 In the last article we went over the differences in DEX V2 and DEX V3 in terms of how liquidity functions, but in this article we will focus on what it looks like to be a liquidity provider. DEX V2 In DEX V2, new liquidity is added in a 50/50 value split at whatever pool ratio is currently trading. If a user wants to provide $100 of FLR to the FLR/HLN LP, they must also add $100 of HLN to balance the position.
